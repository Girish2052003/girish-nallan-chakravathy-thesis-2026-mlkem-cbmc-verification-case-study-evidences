# Reuse and licensing position

This evidence-spine package does **not** select or impose a software, documentation or data licence on the repository. The author must choose the repository-level reuse terms deliberately and must not license third-party or upstream material beyond the rights already granted by its original licence.

Before the thesis evidence release is frozen:

1. review the licence and notice files in the frozen `mlkem-native` snapshot and all other incorporated third-party material;
2. decide separate reuse terms where appropriate for author-created documentation, harnesses, scripts and research records;
3. retain all upstream copyright and licence notices;
4. state clearly which directories contain third-party snapshots and which contain author-created research evidence; and
5. obtain institutional or supervisory guidance if the intended licence treatment is uncertain.

Absence of a repository licence normally does not grant general reuse permission. Public visibility and reproducible inspection are therefore distinct from permission to redistribute or modify the material.
