# AI-Assisted Documentation and Author Responsibility Notice

AI assistance was used primarily to organise evidence, improve clarity, consolidate information, and express the author’s work in a consistent and readable form.

The AI system did not independently establish that any verification property was true. Where CBMC reports successful verification, that result applies only to the explicitly encoded properties, assumptions, models, bounds, tool configuration, and source revision recorded for that experiment. The AI-assisted summaries describe and interpret those recorded results but do not replace the formal-tool evidence.

The repository author defined the verification objectives, supervised the experiments, reviewed the generated artefacts and tool outputs, resolved identified issues, selected the final evidence, and accepted responsibility for the published interpretations and conclusions.

If an AI-assisted explanation conflicts with the underlying source files, commands, manifests, checksums, or CBMC outputs, the directly recorded and checksum-bound experimental evidence is authoritative.

The use of AI assistance is disclosed to support academic transparency and an accurate distinction between documentation support and formal-tool verification.
