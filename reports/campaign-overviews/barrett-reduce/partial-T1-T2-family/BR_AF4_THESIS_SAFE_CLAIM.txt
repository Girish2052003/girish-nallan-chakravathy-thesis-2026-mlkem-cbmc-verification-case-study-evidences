BR-AF4 FINAL THESIS-SAFE CLAIM

At frozen mlkem-native commit af4c5abdd5958bdc65a03cd5ee86708264f93304, CBMC 6.9.0 verified that the
actual C implementation of mlk_barrett_reduce refines an independently
expressed centered reduction modulo 3329 over the complete int16_t input
domain. The checked properties include centered range, modular congruence,
exact oracle equality, uniqueness of the closest int16_t representative,
fixed-point behaviour on centered inputs, idempotence, and invariance across
congruent int16_t inputs.

The campaign used no input assumptions for the main full-domain obligations,
did not modify the frozen production source, did not replace the target body
with a contract, and retained the real implementation arithmetic in the GOTO
models. Separate boundary controls and implementation/oracle mutations were
used to provide non-vacuity and sensitivity evidence.

These results do not constitute a new theorem in modular arithmetic, a new
Barrett-reduction algorithm, or a universal portability proof. The contribution
is an implementation-specific and reproducible CBMC verification campaign for
the examined frozen mlkem-native C body. The signed-right-shift claim is limited
to the semantics represented by the recorded CBMC environment.
