# Portability notes

Some original candidate sidecars and nested stage manifests preserve absolute
origin paths. Their stored hashes were independently validated.

This complete family package provides:

- basename-only sidecars for all five theorem archives;
- a relative top-level SHA-256 manifest;
- an explicit frozen-source identity;
- individual final forensic verdicts.

The original evidence is preserved byte-for-byte inside its respective final
archive.
