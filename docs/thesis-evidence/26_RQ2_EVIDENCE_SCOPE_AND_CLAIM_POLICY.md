# RQ2 evidence scope and claim policy

## RQ2

> To what extent can deterministic workflow controls govern AI-assisted formal-verification work without constraining the reasoning component’s semantic autonomy, and what architectural limits emerge when such supervision is transferred from a stateless LLM head to a repository-aware coding agent?

## Permitted evidence-level claims

The evidence may support, when the corresponding locator is resolved:

- the observed architecture and control allocation of V1–V4;
- replayability and engineering/regression readiness where directly recorded;
- genuine API-backed semantic candidate generation in V3/V4 where raw API validation exists;
- bounded failure/non-completion states and their immediate recorded loci;
- the distinction between engineering readiness and semantic lifecycle completion;
- the classification of the two preserved V4 directory states as one V4 architecture with a utility/backend increment;
- V5 as a reallocation, not elimination, of deterministic control;
- the V5 skill experiment as a secondary capability-distillation probe.

## Prohibited or unsupported claims

The thesis must not claim:

- preservation of “100%” of Codex capability or any numerical percentage of capability;
- causal proof that deterministic control caused V3/V4 non-completion;
- universal failure of deterministic orchestration;
- intrinsic superiority of Codex over the stateless LLM head;
- causal superiority of V5 over V4;
- that 53/53 or 56/56 engineering regressions establish live semantic verification success;
- that the offline/simulated Codex integration packet is evidence of a real authenticated host experiment;
- individual-skill causal effectiveness, general skill superiority or numerical efficiency improvement;
- full correctness of ML-KEM, complete correctness of the investigated functions, or global novelty.

## V4 identity rule

`v4-evaluated-second-generation-llm-integrated-workflow` and `v4-codex-skills-scientific-experiment-baseline` are preserved evidence states of **one V4 architecture generation**. The latter records a Codex backend/utility increment. Historical directory names may be used as locators but must not be counted as separate architecture generations.
