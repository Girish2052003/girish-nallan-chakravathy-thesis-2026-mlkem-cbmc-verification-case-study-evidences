# Limitations and non-claims

This campaign does not establish:

- complete correctness of all ML-KEM;
- end-to-end cryptographic correctness;
- correctness for arbitrary mathematical integers outside the recorded
  signed-representability condition;
- correctness of assembly implementations;
- correctness for every compiler, platform or build configuration;
- constant-time behaviour or side-channel resistance;
- universal correctness beyond the frozen finite CBMC model.

Mutation testing strengthens non-vacuity and sensitivity evidence, but it does
not independently prove correctness.
