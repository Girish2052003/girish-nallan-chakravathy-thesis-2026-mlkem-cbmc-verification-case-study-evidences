# BR-AF4-T4 Novelty Boundary

T4 does not claim a new principle of modular arithmetic and does not claim
that the Barrett-reduction algorithm itself is new.

T4 studies a parameter-design question for the exact arithmetic structure
used by the frozen implementation:

Within the complete nonnegative multiplier interval for which
M*a + 2^25 is representable as signed int32_t for every int16_t input,
which multipliers implement exact centered reduction modulo 3329 over
the complete input domain?

The proposed result is that 20159 is the unique such multiplier.

A frozen-repository overlap audit is recorded during this run. A documented
external literature search remains required before a qualified originality
statement is used. An unconditional world-first claim is prohibited.
