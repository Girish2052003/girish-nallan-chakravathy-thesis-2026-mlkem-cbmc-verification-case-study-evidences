     4	 * Correct determinism theorem:

     6	 *   equal complete polynomial values

     8	 *   equal complete message outputs.
     9	 *
    10	 * Deliberately corrupted theorem:

    12	 *   two polynomial values differ at exactly one coefficient k,

    17	 *

    21	 * CBMC must reject complete output equality.

    27	#include <stdint.h>
