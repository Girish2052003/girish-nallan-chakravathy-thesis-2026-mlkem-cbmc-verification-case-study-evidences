ROOT_CAUSE:
The coverage command supplied --cover and --unwinding-assertions together.
CBMC 6.9.0 explicitly rejects this option combination.

Retain the exact explicit --unwindset bounds but remove
--unwinding-assertions. Execute coverage through CBMC only.
