     2	 * MSG-T2-R3A — same-decision invariance.
     3	 *
     4	 * For arbitrary canonical polynomials A and B and arbitrary k:
     5	 *
     6	 *   Compress1(A[k]) == Compress1(B[k])
     7	 *
     8	 * implies:
     9	 *
    10	 *   bit_k(tomsg(A)) == bit_k(tomsg(B)).
    11	 *
    12	 * A[k] and B[k] need not be numerically equal. Every coefficient outside k
    13	 * also remains unrestricted between A and B.
    14	 *
    15	 * The decision predicate is expressed independently as the exact canonical
    16	 * interval established for Compress1:
    17	 *
    18	 *   decision(u) = 1 iff 833 <= u <= 2496.
    19	 *
    20	 * This is a functional invariance theorem. It is not a timing,
    21	 * microarchitectural, leakage, or constant-time claim.
    22	 */
