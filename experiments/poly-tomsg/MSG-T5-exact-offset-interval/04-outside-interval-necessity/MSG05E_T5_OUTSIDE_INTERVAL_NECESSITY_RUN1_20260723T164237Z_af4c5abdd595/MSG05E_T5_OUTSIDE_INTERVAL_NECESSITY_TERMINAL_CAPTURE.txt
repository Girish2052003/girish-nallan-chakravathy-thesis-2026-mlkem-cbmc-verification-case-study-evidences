     2	 * MSG-T5 exact admissible-offset interval — necessity theorem.
     3	 *
     4	 * Let c be an arbitrary uint32 offset outside:
     5	 *
     6	 *   [1073417800, 1074063871].
     7	 *
     8	 * This harness chooses a canonical counterexample coefficient according to the
     9	 * complete outside-domain partition:
    10	 *
    11	 *   Region 1: 0 <= c < 1073417800
    12	 *             witness u = 2497
    13	 *
    14	 *   Region 2: 1074063871 < c < 2^31
    15	 *             witness u = 832

    17	 *   Region 3: 2^31 <= c <= UINT32_MAX

    20	 * It proves that the source-faithful parameterized model disagrees with the
    21	 * independent canonical threshold oracle for the chosen witness.

    23	 * Together with MSG05D sufficiency, this proves exactness of the closed

    37	{

    45	      (uint64_t)MSG_T5_FIXED_MULTIPLIER;

    50	          (uint64_t)0xFFFFFFFFu);
