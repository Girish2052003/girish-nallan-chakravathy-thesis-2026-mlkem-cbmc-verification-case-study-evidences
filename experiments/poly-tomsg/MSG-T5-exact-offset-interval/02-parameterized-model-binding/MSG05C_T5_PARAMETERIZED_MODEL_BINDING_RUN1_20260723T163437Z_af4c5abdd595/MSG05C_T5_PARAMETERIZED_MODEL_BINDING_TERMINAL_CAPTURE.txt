     2	 * MSG-T5 model-binding theorem.
     3	 *
     4	 * The real frozen helper computes:
     5	 *
     6	 *   d0 = uint32(u * 1290168)
     7	 *   return uint8((d0 + 2^30) >> 31)

    13	 *
    14	 *   1. the real frozen mlk_scalar_compress_d1 helper; and

    56	      (uint32_t)(
