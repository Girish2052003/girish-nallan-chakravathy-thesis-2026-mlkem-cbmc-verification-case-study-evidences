RELATION:
Input B equals input A except for one selected canonical coefficient.
The selected coefficient in B equals the selected coefficient in A plus one.

CORE_OBLIGATIONS=4

PBYTES-T2.P1:
Even coefficient, low byte not 255.
Byte zero increments by one; bytes one and two remain unchanged.

PBYTES-T2.P2:
Even coefficient, low byte equal to 255.
Byte zero wraps to zero; the low nibble of byte one increments;
the upper nibble of byte one and byte two remain unchanged.

PBYTES-T2.P3:
Odd coefficient, low nibble not 15.
The upper nibble of byte one increments by 0x10;
its lower nibble and the other two bytes remain unchanged.

PBYTES-T2.P4:
Odd coefficient, low nibble equal to 15.
The upper nibble of byte one wraps from 0xF0 to zero;
byte two increments by one; byte zero and the lower nibble remain unchanged.

INPUT_DOMAIN:
All coefficients of both inputs are canonical:
0 <= coefficient < MLKEM_Q.
