0035: ## PBYTES-T3 — Exact canonical image and invalid-codeword exclusion
0036: 
0037: The independent decoder interprets each three-byte block arithmetically as
0038: W = b0 + 256*b1 + 65536*b2, with fields W mod 4096 and W / 4096.
0039: It must not call mlk_poly_frombytes.
0040: 
0041: 1. PBYTES-T3.P1 — every produced even field is below MLKEM_Q.
0042: 2. PBYTES-T3.P2 — every produced odd field is below MLKEM_Q.
0043: 3. PBYTES-T3.P3 — every canonical 24-bit block is realizable.
0044: 4. PBYTES-T3.P4 — a block with either invalid field is not realizable.
0045: 5. PBYTES-T3.P5 — full-array image iff all 256 fields are canonical.
