INDEPENDENT_ARITHMETIC_DECODER:
W = b0 + 256*b1 + 65536*b2
even_coefficient = W mod 4096
odd_coefficient = W / 4096

PBYTES-T4.P1:
For every canonical polynomial and every pair position, independently
decoding the actual output block recovers the original even coefficient.

PBYTES-T4.P2:
For every canonical polynomial and every pair position, independently
decoding the actual output block recovers the original odd coefficient.

PBYTES-T4.P3:
For any two canonical polynomials and every pair position, their output
blocks at that position are equal if and only if their corresponding
coefficient pairs are equal.

PBYTES-T4.P4:
For any two complete canonical polynomials, equality of all 384 output
bytes implies equality of all 256 input coefficients.

P4_LOGICAL_FORM:
serialize(A) = serialize(B) implies A = B.
